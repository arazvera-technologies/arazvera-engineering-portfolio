# L6-RENEW-001 — Renewable Deal Preflight
**ARAZVERA TECHNOLOGIES | Level 6 Specialist Buyer Catalog | v0.1.0 | 16 November 2026**

**Product status:** Offline, proprietary-source, synthetic laboratory prototype; not customer-commissioned, certified or production-deployed.

## The buyer problem
Renewable-energy platforms, technical advisers and project owners may receive inconsistent descriptions of Solar PV, BESS and hybrid investment opportunities. A **pre-introduction evidence preflight** can identify missing data and conflicting claims for human reviewers before a project is shared with a prospective investor. This is a technical data-quality workflow, not an investment evaluation.

## Available functions in v0.1
- Strict JSON opportunity schema validation: missing/invalid fields, duplicate IDs and oversized inputs.
- Separate units for solar MWp, BESS power MW and battery energy MWh; flag calculated storage-duration review cases.
- Detect conflicts between declared "ready to build" stage and stated permit, land-right or grid-connection status.
- Identify missing, expired or stale **evidence references**. References are not authenticated documents.
- Generate deterministic machine-readable JSON and reviewer-friendly Markdown reports.
- Illustrative matching between **fictional** project and investor criteria; every proposed match requires human review. No contact or transactions.
- Local-only CLI execution without paid APIs or third-party dependency installation.

## Technical evidence
**55/55 automated tests passed in local runs, including on Windows Python 3.12.** The prototype includes four fictional renewable-project records and two illustrative synthetic investor-match cases. This new product has **no buyer acceptance, third-party certification or public CI claim**.
