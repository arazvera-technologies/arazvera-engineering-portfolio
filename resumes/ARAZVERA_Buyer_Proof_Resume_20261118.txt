A controlled four-step engagement
1. Written brief
- sanitized inputs/outputs, authorized platforms, versions and required outcomes.
2. Scoped fit
- show the matching starter, explain missing integrations, set milestones, source/licence rights and payment eligibility.
3. Evidence-backed build
- develop authorized adapters and regression, permission, failure and environment tests.
4. Handover
- deliver only contracted source or artifacts, configuration templates, reproducible results and runbooks after acceptance.
