# Level 7 — Written Scoping and Acceptance Checklist

ARAZVERA uses **asynchronous written English only**. This is a qualification aid, not a proposal or legal agreement.

Before any source evaluation or paid work, the buyer and ARAZVERA should jointly document:

1. **Precise problem and owner:** Which business process fails, who owns it, and what measurable improvement is sought?
2. **Existing stack:** What are the already-approved ERP, AI orchestration, or database tools? Which integration is currently missing?
3. **Allowed data:** Can the pilot use fabricated or carefully redacted examples? No production data or live credentials are required for the first scoping step.
4. **Acceptance evidence:** What exact tests, example outputs, rejections, failure handling and documentation are required?
5. **Deployment ownership:** Buyer purchases/administers any VPS, API usage, domains, accounts, databases and other paid resources in its own name.
6. **Security roles:** Who authorizes the tool, reviews approval decisions, administers backups and may access the resulting data?
7. **IP and rights:** Is the buyer requesting a limited nonexclusive license, customized source, OEM rights or assignment? Terms depend on existing rights/provenance audit.
8. **Regulatory restrictions:** Have both parties verified lawful cross-border work, source export, data protection, payment channels and sanctions for the seller's **actual residence in Iran**?
9. **Collaboration modality:** Buyer explicitly accepts **written-only, asynchronous English** correspondence, written technical explanations and test reports—**no calls, video or live interviews**.
10. **Commercial milestone:** Agree a paid bounded phase with clear deliverables and written acceptance. Production hardening and support are separately scoped.

No customer use, fixed fee, certification, SLA or production readiness is implied by this checklist.
