## Commercial delivery model

1. **Written discovery:** buyer describes the operational problem, inputs, acceptance criteria and authorized environment.
2. **Scoped engineering quote:** deliverables, exclusions, IP/source rights and maintenance are agreed before any transfer.
3. **Synthetic-first evaluation:** demonstrate local/private prototypes without buyer secrets; propose a clear adapter and regression plan.
4. **Client-specific implementation:** buyer provides its own infrastructure, permitted APIs, licences and required credentials through approved channels.
5. **Staging, verification and handover:** acceptance tests and security checks in the authorized target environment before production use.

**Readiness:** Available for technical review and scope negotiation. Not every module is production-ready, sale-cleared, customer-accepted or a minor configuration change away from deployment. Real IAM, regulated data, independent security testing, recovery objectives and integrations may require substantial additional work.

**Disclosure:** No private source, archives, API keys, personal customer data or hidden deployment access is offered in this public catalog. Every cross-border engagement must comply with applicable contracting, licensing, export/sanctions, data and lawful-payment rules. The owner works from Iran; engagement is asynchronous written English, not phone/video interviews.
